About Us

Rorya United FC is a football club established with the goal of developing and nurturing youth talents in Rorya District. This club has been a hub for sports development by providing opportunities for youth to showcase their abilities and participate in various local and regional competitions.

Our Vision

To be the best football club in Mara Region and one of the leading clubs in nurturing youth talents in the country, from district to regional to national level.

Our Founder

Peter Owino is the sports leader and youth development advocate in Rorya District. He is the owner of Rorya United FC, which he founded on 01/June/2024.
